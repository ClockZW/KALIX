What we do

Aluminium and glass, done properly.

From one bathroom mirror to a full shopfront, we handle the measuring, the making and the fitting.

Aluminium Windows

Casement, sliding and top-hung windows in white, bronze, charcoal or silver frames.

Sliding & Hinged Doors

Patio sliders, folding doors and entrance doors that open smoothly and lock securely.

Shopfronts

Clean, bright aluminium-and-glass fronts for shops, offices and showrooms.

Shower Cubicles

Framed and frameless shower screens in clear or frosted toughened glass.

Mirrors & Wardrobes

Sliding mirror wardrobes, gym and salon mirrors, and bathroom mirrors cut to size.

Balustrades & Railings

Glass and aluminium balustrades for stairs, balconies and verandas.

Office Partitions

Aluminium partitions with glass panels to divide space without losing light.

Glass Cutting & Repairs

Broken pane? We cut and replace glass, fix rollers, locks and worn seals.
